# Wicked
It is a *Broadway Musical* telling the backstory of Oz through the friendship of two sorcery roommates: 

- Elphaba, a green-skinned outcast who rebels after discovering the Wizard is a corrupt fraud. She becomes the **Wicked Witch of the West**
-  Glinda, a popular blonde who chooses power over defiance. She becomes **Glinda the Good**
